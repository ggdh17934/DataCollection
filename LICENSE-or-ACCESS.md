# License and Access Terms

## Dataset Access

This dataset is a **private academic collection** created for the Master's coursework at the University of Echahid Hamma Lakhdar – El Oued, Department of Computer Science, Academic Year 2026–2027.

## Access Restrictions

- This repository is **private** and accessible only to the student author and supervisor.
- The dataset is not released for public distribution at this time.
- All images were originally captured by the student during the project period.
- No third-party copyrighted images are included.

## Rights Statement

- All photographs are original works captured by the dataset author.
- No identifiable individuals appear in any image.
- EXIF/geolocation metadata has been stripped from all images.
- Scenes were photographed in locations where the author had permission to capture images.
- No images of public infrastructure, restricted areas, or private property without consent are included.

## Consent Records

Consent records (if any) are stored privately and are NOT included in this repository per project guidelines.

## Third-Party Material

No third-party datasets or images are included in this collection. The comparable benchmarks (LEVIR-CD, CDnet) are referenced for comparison purposes only — no samples from those datasets appear in this collection.
