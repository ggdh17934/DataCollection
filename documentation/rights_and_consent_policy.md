# Rights and Consent Policy

## Data Collection Rights

### Image Capture Permissions
- All images are captured in locations where the student has explicit permission to photograph
- Indoor scenes: Student's own residence, or university spaces with verbal/written permission
- Outdoor scenes: Public spaces where photography is permitted, or private spaces with owner consent

### Object Ownership
- All objects manipulated (added, removed, moved) during scene changes are either:
  - Owned by the student, OR
  - Used with the owner's verbal permission
- No public or essential infrastructure is modified

### No Human Subjects
- No identifiable individuals appear in any image
- No faces, names, personal documents, or identifying information
- If a person accidentally appears in a capture, the image is re-taken or discarded

### Metadata Privacy
- All EXIF/geolocation metadata is stripped from images before repository upload
- Site names use generic descriptions (e.g., "indoor_kitchen") rather than precise addresses
- Camera serial numbers and device IDs are removed

## Consent Records
- Consent records for site access (if any) are stored privately
- They are NOT uploaded to the GitHub repository
- Available upon supervisor request

## Third-Party Material
- No images from existing datasets are used in this collection
- LEVIR-CD and CDnet are referenced for comparison only — zero samples copied

## Minors Policy
- No minors are photographed as per handbook requirements
- If minors are present at a capture site, they are excluded from the frame or capture is postponed
